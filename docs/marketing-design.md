# Make the evidence useful to a buying team

The conversion goal is a relevant comparison request from a verified work mailbox. Existing research stays open. Visitors should be able to understand the result, challenge it, and share the exact evidence without giving up an email address.

## Implemented

- Buyer-oriented headline and two paths: explore the evidence or test a shortlist.
- Official Alhena logo and current blue, lavender, teal and warm-orange colors.
- Interactive shopping/support charts using the existing live evidence, including every storefront and the rubric dimensions behind the means.
- Shareable report sections, lane selections and individual conversations; branded social preview images.
- Contextual request link carrying provider names into the existing email-verification flow.
- Clear explanation of the method, operator, sample limits and review process.

## Distribution after launch

Share a useful finding with a direct evidence link. Invite readers to inspect the answer and request the comparison their team needs. Avoid claiming a universal winner from this pilot. With permission and sufficient evidence, future category-specific reports can provide a reason to return. Do not promise a publishing cadence that the evaluation process cannot support.

Measure report visits, evidence exploration, requests started, verified submissions and approved comparisons before claiming marketing impact. This refresh adds no third-party trackers or marketing email subscription. Transactional request updates retain their existing purpose.

Reference patterns: https://evals.gorgias.com/ (clear method, visible sample, interactive results) and https://alhena.ai/ (current brand palette and logo). No competitor ranking or unsupported speed/automation metric was imported.
